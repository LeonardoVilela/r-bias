# Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, evaluate ideas on their technical and scientific merits, and avoid harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or disclosure of private information. Discussions of race and racism must use precise language and recognize that the benchmark's categories are social and administrative categories, not biological essences.

Research disagreements should identify evidence, assumptions, and possible effects on reproducibility. Reports involving security, privacy, harmful benchmark use, or accidental credential exposure should not be posted publicly before maintainers can assess them.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, racial stereotyping directed at participants, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and retaliation against good-faith reports.
